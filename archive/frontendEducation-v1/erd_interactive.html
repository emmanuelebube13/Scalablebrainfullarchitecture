🏛️ Architectural Schema Modeling: Why Scalable Brain is a Hybrid Fact Constellation

Primary Pattern: Fact Constellation (Galaxy Schema)

Scalable Brain does not use a single flat Star Schema. Instead, it anchors 8 analytical fact tables around two shared conformed dimension hubs (dim_asset and dim_strategy), while branching into a normalized Snowflake sub-hierarchy for versioned parameters and currency universe bridges.

🌌 Fact Constellation (Galaxy) Active Core

Multiple independent fact tables at different grains (candlestick bars, regime states, simulated trades, attribution runs, live orders) share conformed dimensions dim_asset and dim_strategy.

❄️ Snowflake Sub-Hierarchy Normalized

dim_strategy normalizes outward into dim_strategy_config (JSON parameter hashes) and bridge table dim_strategy_asset_mapping (resolving M:N currency pairs).

⭐ Classic Star Schema 1 Fact : N Dims

Why SB departs: A strict Star Schema only permits one central fact table. Forced denormalization of 14.34M price bars with 111.5k trade outcomes would cause massive row explosion.

⚙️ Operational 3NF & Contracts Stateful

Tables like fact_job_runs, fact_market_prices_quarantine, and regime_strategy_map.json act as real-time operational guardrails and fail-closed routing contracts.
