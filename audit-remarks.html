2. Core Auditor Remarks & Remediations

Remark A: Dual-Identity Ambiguity Resolved

Historical Flaw: System 1 generated signals with synthetic UUIDs, while OANDA returned numeric order IDs (e.g. #10294), leaving the database unable to perform joins between strategy parameters and actual execution slippage.
Remediation: Added foreign key correlation in trade_journal binding trade_id, decision_id, and signal_id. Every row now preserves full end-to-end provenance.

Remark B: True Net Currency Exposure vs Gross Position Limits

Historical Flaw: The system enforced a naive 10-slot limit. A trader could hold Long EUR_USD, Long GBP_USD, Long AUD_USD, Short USD_JPY, and Short USD_CHF — effectively taking a 500% directional short USD position that violated risk limits while appearing "diversified".
Remediation: Implemented True Net Currency Exposure in System 3 Guardian Stage E. Every position decomposes into base and quote components in account currency (CAD), enforcing a strict max_correlated_exposure_pct = 20% cap per currency (CHF, EUR, USD, GBP, AUD, JPY, CAD).

Remark C: SQLite WAL Concurrency & Read-Only Replica Isolation

Historical Flaw: Telemetry dashboard queries caused database is locked errors during high-frequency AMS Guardian risk evaluations.
Remediation: Applied SQLite WAL (Write-Ahead Logging) mode across all local databases with busy_timeout=5000. Telemetry backend connects strictly with read-only flags (?mode=ro&immutable=1), ensuring zero read-write contention.

3. Schema Topology: Fact Constellation (Galaxy Schema)

Scalable Brain employs a Fact Constellation (Galaxy) Schema rather than a single Star Schema because algorithmic quantitative trading spans distinct business grains:

Pattern Structure Fit for Algorithmic Trading
Star Schema Single central fact table with flat dimensions Poor. Fails because 14.3M market candle bars cannot share the same grain as 1,248 executed live orders or 84 circuit breaker events.
Snowflake Schema Normalized dimension hierarchies (Asset -> Currency -> Central Bank) Excessive join overhead for sub-second risk calculations; slows down time-critical Guardian Stage A evaluations.
Galaxy Schema (Active) Multiple independent fact tables sharing conformed dimensions (dim_asset, dim_strategy) Optimal. Clean separation of concerns between market ingestion, strategy scoring, risk approvals, broker fills, and post-trade attribution.
