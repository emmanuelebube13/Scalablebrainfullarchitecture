System 3 Guardian Temporal Risk Rules

Automated temporal checks enforced during Stage T of the AMS risk pipeline.

Friday Weekend Close Cutoff

Mandatory Freeze
Friday 20:55 UTC (Market Closes 21:00 UTC)

All new order submissions are strictly rejected 5 minutes before the weekend close. Prevents holding unhedged breakout entries exposed to unforeseen weekend geopolitical gap risk. Existing swing positions with stops in place are permitted to carry over based on strategy parameters.

Sunday Open Spread Buffer

Stabilization Window
Sunday 21:00 – 22:15 UTC (75-minute quarantine)

When broker liquidity pools re-open on Sunday, interbank spreads routinely widen by 10x to 25x. The Guardian holds all System 1 candidate signals in queue until 22:15 UTC when average spreads contract below the maximum permissible threshold (1.8 pips for EUR_USD).

Daily Rollover Financing Shield

Overnight Rollover
Daily 21:00 – 21:15 UTC (Swap Window)

Broker daily rollover swap rates are calculated at 5:00 PM EST (21:00 UTC). To avoid getting filled at artificial spread spikes, signal evaluations are paused for 15 minutes while financing charges are journaled into fact_live_trades.

London / New York Liquidity Overlap

Prime Execution
Daily 12:00 – 16:00 UTC (4-Hour Peak Window)

Represents approximately 70% of global daily forex turnover. Spreads reach institutional minimums (0.1–0.4 pips). System 1 Trend-Following and Momentum strategies (e.g. precision_swing, bb_midline_break) execute with full Kelly authorization.
