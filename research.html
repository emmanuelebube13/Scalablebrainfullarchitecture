Mathematical frameworks, statistical formulations, and empirical methodologies governing edge discovery, meta-labeling, position sizing, and regime qualification in Scalable Brain.

Guiding Axiom

1. Deterministic Mathematical Edges vs. Black-Box Prophecy

Scalable Brain is built on one fundamental quantitative principle: algorithmic trading systems must encode deterministic structural edges, not end-to-end black-box predictions. Machine learning is a tool for context qualification and sizing calibration, not for forecasting tomorrow's price.

Why Price Forecasting Fails

Direct price prediction (e.g. LSTM predicting next close) is overwhelmed by non-stationarity, regime shifts, and low signal-to-noise ratios in financial time series. It invariably overfits noise.

The Structural Edge Approach

Encode structural market phenomena (liquidity sweeps, volatility expansion, mean-reversion at support/resistance). ML models only decide when that specific phenomenon exhibits a statistical probability of success.

Marcos López de Prado Methodology

2. Meta-Labeling & Triple Barrier Method

Following de Prado's Advances in Financial Machine Learning, System 1 decouples trade direction from trade execution confidence:

Primary Model (Rule-Based Strategy): Generates Direction \(D \in \{-1, +1\}\) with fixed Triple Barriers (Upper Take-Profit \(TP\), Lower Stop-Loss \(SL\), and Time Horizon \(T\)).

Secondary Model (XGBoost Meta-Labeler): Predicts Binary Outcome \(Y \in \{0, 1\}\):
\(Y = 1 \iff\) Upper barrier \(TP\) is touched before \(SL\) or \(T\).
\(P(Y=1 \mid X)\) yields model confidence score \(\in [0.0, 1.0]\).

If \(P(Y=1) < 0.55\), the candidate signal is filtered out at the factory stage, protecting capital from low-conviction market chops.

3. Fractional Kelly Criterion with Negative-Edge Clamping

System 3 Guardian scales position units dynamically using the continuous Kelly formula calibrated by out-of-sample win rates and payoff ratios:

\(f^* = \frac{p \cdot b - (1 - p)}{b}\)

Where:
• \(p\) = Calibrated win probability from S1 meta-labeler and Bayesian historical update.
• \(b\) = Payoff ratio \(\frac{\text{Average Win } R}{\text{Average Loss } R}\).
• \(c = 0.25\) (Quarter-Kelly multiplier to eliminate catastrophic gambler's ruin).
• Negative-Edge Clamp: If \(p \cdot b - (1 - p) \le 0\), then \(f^* = 0\) (Immediate rejection).
Market Dynamics

4. Hidden Markov Model (HMM) Structural Regimes

Markets alternate between non-stationary states. System 1 fits a Gaussian HMM on normalized logarithmic returns and realized volatility to label 4 distinct market regimes:

1. Bullish Momentum

Positive drift, low-to-moderate volatility. Favors Trend-Following (precision_swing).

2. Bearish Momentum

Negative drift, elevated volatility. Short trend continuations.

3. Mean-Reverting Range

Zero drift, low volatility. Favors Mean-Reversion (bb_midline_break, rsi_stoch_confluence).

4. High Volatility Chop

Erratic drift, extreme variance. Guardian halts trend entries; activates volatility breakouts only.
