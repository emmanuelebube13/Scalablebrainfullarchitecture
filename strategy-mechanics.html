Family 1: Trend-Following & Directional Momentum (31 Strategies)

Live Qualified Core

The Economic Edge: FX exchange rates exhibit significant medium-term autocorrelation driven by macroeconomic interest-rate differentials and central bank monetary policy cycles. Trend strategies do not predict reversals; they capture the sustained meat of a multi-week macroeconomic move.

MATHEMATICAL INDICATORS
• Fast/Slow Exponential Moving Averages: \(\text{EMA}_{50}\) and \(\text{EMA}_{200}\).
• Average Directional Index: \(\text{ADX}(14) > 25\) (ensures directional trend, filters chop).
• Average True Range (ATR): \(\text{ATR}(14)\) volatility normalization.
ENTRY & BRACKETING MECHANICS
• Entry: Price pulls back to touch \(\text{EMA}_{50}\) while remaining strictly above \(\text{EMA}_{200}\) with bullish candle close.
• Stop Loss: Set structurally at \(\text{Swing Low} - 1.5 \times \text{ATR}(14)\).
• Take Profit: Trailed dynamically at \(2.5 \times \text{ATR}(14)\) or \(\text{EMA}_{200}\) touch.

Family 2: Mean-Reversion & Statistical Arbitrage (9 Strategies)

Range-Bound Specialist

The Economic Edge: Approximately 70% of foreign exchange trading sessions occur in non-trending, range-bound states. In these periods, price deviations beyond 2.0 standard deviations reflect transient liquidity exhaustion rather than genuine structural repricing.

MATHEMATICAL INDICATORS
• Bollinger Bands: 20-period Simple Moving Average with \(\pm 2.2\) standard deviation bands.
• Relative Strength Index (RSI): \(\text{RSI}(14)\) with overbought (>70) and oversold (<30) zones.
• Stochastic Oscillator: \(\%K(14, 3, 3)\) crossover confirmation.
ENTRY & BRACKETING MECHANICS
• Entry: Price pierces the outer Bollinger Band (\(+2.2\sigma\)), then prints a reversal candle closing back inside the band with RSI hook.
• Stop Loss: Fixed tight barrier at \(1.2 \times \text{ATR}(14)\) beyond the extreme wick.
• Take Profit: 20-period SMA Midline touch (high win-rate, quick turnover).

Family 3: Volatility & Range Breakout (15 Strategies)

Expansion Specialist

The Economic Edge: Volatility is cyclical. Protracted periods of tight price consolidation (low volatility compression) inevitably transition into explosive directional breakouts as resting order liquidity is triggered.

MATHEMATICAL INDICATORS
• Donchian Channels: Highest High and Lowest Low over 20 and 55 periods.
• Keltner Channels & Bollinger Squeeze: BB width inside Keltner band signals compression.
• Historical Volatility Ratio: \(\frac{\text{ATR}(5)}{\text{ATR}(20)} < 0.70\) (identifies coiling).
ENTRY & BRACKETING MECHANICS
• Entry: Price closes above the 20-period Donchian Upper Channel with ATR expansion.
• Stop Loss: Opposite 20-period Lower Channel or \(2.0 \times \text{ATR}(14)\).
• Take Profit: Asymmetric \(3.0 \times \text{ATR}(14)\) or parabolic trailing stop.

Family 4: Liquidity Sweeps & Structural Levels (6 Strategies)

Institutional Flow

The Economic Edge: Retail breakout orders place clustered stop-losses just above obvious swing highs and below swing lows. Institutional market makers engineer liquidity sweeps through these levels to fill large institutional orders, followed by immediate price rejection.

MATHEMATICAL INDICATORS
• Rolling High/Low Pivots: Prior 48-hour session highs and lows.
• Wick-to-Body Ratio: Candle wick must exceed 65% of total candle range.
• Volume Exhaustion: Tick volume spike exceeding \(1.8 \times \text{MA}(20)\).
ENTRY & BRACKETING MECHANICS
• Entry: Price pierces prior 48h high by 5–15 pips, fails to close above, and prints a reversal pinbar closing back inside the range.
• Stop Loss: Placed 3 pips above the extreme rejection wick tip.
• Take Profit: Opposite side liquidity pool (previous swing low).
