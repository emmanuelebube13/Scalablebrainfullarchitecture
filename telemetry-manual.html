1. Dashboard Cockpit (`Overview.tsx`)

Active Trading Desk

The primary operational screen. Acts as a low-scroll high-density cockpit displaying live positions, floating risk, execution progress, and direct 1-click candlestick charting without page reloads.

Key Elements & Controls:

Component Function & Meaning Operator Action
Open Positions (Active Book) Live inventory from ams_open_positions joined with S1 attribution. Shows Trade #, Pair, Dir, Strategy, Regime, Units, Entry/Current, SL↔TP progress, Risk @ SL, Live R, floating P&L, Financing, and Age. Click > to expand the 6-card inspector; click 📈 or Pair badge to open in-context candlestick chart modal.
SL ↔ TP Progress Gauge Compact visual progress bar tracking current mid-price relative to Stop Loss (left, red) and Take Profit (right, green). If price approaches SL (>85% to stop), monitor for potential stop slippage or impending exit.
RISK AT SL Currency Toggle Header toggle pill [CAD | CCY | %]. Converts quote currency stop distance into account base currency (CAD) using live mid rates. Default to CAD to directly compare risk against floating P&L. Switch to % to verify that no position exceeds 1.5% NAV risk.
Recent Trades (De-duplicated) Execution history from fact_live_trades. Defaults to Closed trades only (with [Closed | All] toggle) so active inventory is not duplicated. Inspect realized R-multiples, execution slippage, broker fees, and final exit reasons.
6-Card Trade Inspector Six diagnostic panels revealed upon expanding any row: SIG (Signal), PLN (Plan), EXE (Execution), MKT (Inline Chart), P&L (Live state), GOV (Active governance rules). Use GOV to check if the trade is subject to the Friday 20:55 UTC weekend freeze or maximum age decay (>5 days).

How to Read the Strategy Cards:

Column / Indicator What It Measures Operator Interpretation
Backtest Column Out-of-sample performance: Win Rate, Profit Factor (PF), Sharpe, Max Drawdown, and Sample Size ($N$). Represents baseline mathematical expectation under historical walk-forward testing.
Live Column Actual demo/live performance in `fact_live_trades` filtered by that card's specific market regime. Shows net P&L per trade in CAD and initial risk $R$. If $N < 10$, live sample size is too small for statistical certainty.
Delta Indicator (Live vs OOS) Percentage lift or decay between live expectancy and out-of-sample baseline. Green: Outperforming backtest.
Red: Decay detected; candidate for quarantine.
Live Column Unavailable Banner Displays when the database query to `fact_live_trades` fails while the GCS model set succeeds. A database connection drop must never look like "zero live trades". Check PostgreSQL / SQLite service status.
Withdrawn Status System 1 has explicitly retracted the model set due to an expired map or structural retraining. Indicates an intentional decision, not an error. S1 will republish upon completion of weekly retraining.

3. Incubator Panel (`Incubator.tsx`)

Shadow Forward-Testing Engine

The Incubator tracks the Shadow Incubation Store (`shadow_incubator.db`). New strategies and experimental variants execute in real-time simulation against streaming market data without risking account capital.

The Contender Tier Classification:

Tier 1: Production Ready Graduation Candidate

$N \ge 30$ forward trades, Win Rate > 50%, Expectancy $E[R] \ge +0.15\text{ R}$, $p_{\text{random}} < 0.05$, and FP-70% > 55%. Ready to be promoted into System 1 Factory.

Tier 2: Watchlist / Incubating Collecting Evidence

Positive edge metrics but insufficient sample size ($N < 30$) or slightly elevated p-value ($0.05 \le p < 0.10$). Continues shadow execution.

Tier 3: Failing / Reject Quarantined

Negative expectancy, $p_{\text{random}} \ge 0.10$, or FP-70% < 40%. Quarantined or scheduled for deletion from the research lab.

Specialized Incubator Statistical Metrics:

Metric Name Mathematical Definition Passing Threshold
FP-70% Rate (First Profit 70%) Percentage of trades where floating profit touches at least +0.70 R before price touches the initial stop-loss barrier. ≥ 52.0%
p_random (Random Walk p-value) Statistical hypothesis test measuring the probability that the observed win rate and profit distribution arose purely by random chance. < 0.05 (95% confidence)
e_ratio_12b (12-Bar Edge Ratio) Ratio of Maximum Favorable Excursion (MFE) to Maximum Adverse Excursion (MAE) measured across the first 12 bars after trade entry. > 1.30
Locked Expectancy R Theoretical expectancy if a trailing rule moved the stop-loss to breakeven immediately upon touching +1.0 R. > +0.20 R

4. Strategy House (`StrategyHouse.tsx`)

Repository & Architectural Reasoning

Strategy House is the platform's definitive encyclopedia answering: What does each strategy do? How does it enter and exit? And why isn't it trading?

The Three Inviolable Design Rules:

Rule 1: An Empty List is Not an Answer

Rendering an empty list as a blank space asserts that "this strategy uses no indicators", which is a claim the code never made. Every absence names itself: "no v2 module", "not reported in registry", or "no docstring available".

Rule 2: Sample Size Travels With the Headline

A strategy reporting Profit Factor 13.58 on 5 out-of-sample trades is statistical noise. Trade count ($N$) is rendered with equal visual weight beside Profit Factor and Win Rate.

Rule 3: Age is a Reading, Not an Alarm

The catalogue republishes on vetting and retrain runs, not on an arbitrary clock. Catalogue age is displayed neutrally in gray, never flashing red as an alarm.

Operating Sub-Panels:

Sub-Panel Information Surfaced Operator Benefit
Strategy Detail Drawer Exact Python class, parameters (lookbacks, multipliers), indicators list, entry logic, exit logic, and qualified regimes. Zero-ambiguity verification of mathematical rules without digging through Git repositories.
Regime Coverage Matrix Cross-tabulation showing which strategies are approved for each of the 4 structural regimes (Trending-Up, Trending-Down, Ranging, High-Vol). Reveals portfolio blind spots (e.g., if no strategies are active during High-Vol chop).
Passed vs Failed Cells Detailed log of why specific asset/timeframe pairings were rejected during out-of-sample vetting (e.g. max drawdown exceeded, trade count < 10). Prevents re-testing parameters that already failed empirical OOS gates.
Parameter Lab Interactive research tool simulating parameter tweaks (ATR multipliers, EMA spans) across historical bars. Explore edge sensitivity and curve-fitting risks before staging into the Incubator.

True Net Currency Exposure Panel:

Decomposes every position into base and quote components converted into account currency (CAD) to enforce a hard 20% max correlated exposure limit (max_correlated_exposure_pct). Prevents hidden directional clusters (e.g. 6 Long-CHF positions).

6. Operator Daily Rhythm & Runbook Checklist

Morning Inspection (07:00 UTC · London Open)
  1. Check Dashboard Cockpit for open slot count (e.g. 8/10 or 10/10).
  2. Verify all open positions have active Stop Loss coverage (100%).
  3. Inspect Circuit Breaker distance (must be > -1.5% NAV).
  4. Review overnight swap charges in Financing column.
Midday Review (13:00 UTC · NY Overlap)
  1. Verify live candle streaming in inline MKT panel.
  2. Inspect True Net Currency Exposure for CHF, EUR, and USD clusters.
  3. Review recent fills in Recent Trades for slippage anomalies (>1.5 pips).
  4. Check FinBERT calendar for impending high-impact FOMC/CPI releases.
Friday Close Routine (20:45 UTC · Pre-Close)
  1. Confirm Guardian enters FROZEN state at 20:55 UTC (zero new entries).
  2. Verify swing positions carrying over the weekend have wide SL brackets.
  3. Take manual backup snapshot of ams.db and system2.db.
  4. Confirm supervisor daemon logs have rotated properly (<50MB).
